How to play

How a game unfolds, from start to finish

No rule booklet: the voice explains everything as you play. Here is what awaits you.

  1. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

    STEP 1Setting up the game

    2 to 6 players: for each, child (with their age) or adult, an avatar and a colour. Choose the length: 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or an open game. Everyone starts with 1,000 Kounouz.

  2. STEP 2The Path moves the pawns

    On their turn, the player reveals their Path: 1 to 5 squares. No dice, no luck: the same fair sequence for everyone.

  3. STEP 3A question that fits each player

    On a Knowledge square, the voice reads a question suited to the player's age: religion, maths, science, history-geography, logic or money skills. Right answer +100 Kounouz, partly right +50. In religion, an explanation and its source come with the answer.

  4. STEP 4Buying properties

    Maktaba, hotels, restaurants, Umrah agencies… 12 properties to buy. Every player who stops there pays the owner 15% of the price.

  5. STEP 5Family challenges

    Over 90 challenges: move, mime an animal, give a compliment, don't laugh… Plus a duel between two players, a Hassanāt opportunity or a bonus question.

  6. 🤲

    STEP 6Giving and Zakat

    On the Giving square, you can give 30 Kounouz to the Masākīn Fund or to a player, and earn 10 Hassanāt. Whoever keeps more than 1,000 Kounouz for 6 rounds pays Zakat (2.5%).

  7. 🏆

    STEP 7The end and the score

    Final score = Kounouz + properties + Hassanāt × 5. The richest does not always win: someone who gave can come out ahead. The perfect moment to talk about it with the children.

The hardest part will be choosing who goes first.The voice explains everything: no rules to read before you play.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Kounouzi in line with our values?

Kounouzi was designed with respect for Islamic principles: you move without dice or luck, there is no music (only a voice and a few effects you can switch off), children never learn riba but saving, giving and Zakat, and there are no ads or in-app purchases. It has no certification: each family remains the judge of what suits them.

Is Kounouzi available in English?

Yes, the English version is in progress. The game screens, settings, cards, family challenges, Hassanāt cards and the voice guide are in English. The 1,764 questions are being translated and checked: until a question is translated and validated, it is shown in French. Religion explanations are also available in Arabic.

From what age?

From age 5. Questions are sorted by age group (5–6, 7–8, 9–10, 11–12 and 13+; for religion 5–8, 8–10, 10–12, 12–14 and 14+) and a voice reads them out loud: children who cannot read yet can play with the older ones.

My children are different ages, how does it work?

Each player gets their own questions, based on their real age. Then the level adapts to their answers: two right answers in a row and the questions go up a notch, a mistake and they become simpler again. Everyone plays together, each at their own level.

Are the religion questions reliable?

They follow the Qur'an and the Sunnah, and are taken only from our 6 reference books, with nothing added. The source is shown under each answer and everything is reviewed before publication. See our reference books.

What are Hassanāt in the game?

They are game points you earn by giving, sharing or completing a family challenge. They count in the score to show that generosity has value. They are not real spiritual rewards: they are a chance to talk about it with your children.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and access is for life, for the whole family.

How many players, and how long?

2 to 6 players around one screen. A game lasts 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or has no limit, and can be paused.

Do I need the internet?

Yes, a connection is needed to open the game and for the voice.

I lost my code, what can I do?

The code stays open on the devices where you have already entered it. Otherwise, write to us with the email address used for the payment and we will send it to you again.
