The problem with classic Islamic quizzes

Questions follow one another, often the same for every age. The eldest wins, the youngest gives up, and everyone ends up on their own screen. You learn little, and nobody wants to play again.

What Kounouzi changes

  • Each player gets their own question: a 6-year-old and a parent never get the same difficulty.
  • Sourced questions: each religion question follows the Qur'an and the Sunnah, comes only from our 7 reference books and shows its source, and an Arabic explanation comes with the answer.
  • The family decides: right answer, partly right, or not this time. Parents stay in charge and explain.
  • No repeats: the game remembers the questions already asked to each player.
  • A board, challenges, giving: questions are part of a real board game.

The English translation of the questions is in progress: untranslated questions appear in French for now.

Examples of religion topics

The pillars of Islam and of faith, the prophets, the Sira of the Prophet ﷺ, Ramadan, good manners (adab), Qur'an vocabulary, based on our 7 reference books. Questions range from the simplest for ages 5–8 to the deepest for teenagers and adults.
