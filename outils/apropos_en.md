# Kounouzi, a family story first and foremost.

### It all began on a simple holiday afternoon…

**As-salāmu ʿalaykum!**

I'm Abou Salmaan, dad of four children, Allahumma baarik.

If there is one thing I love in life, it's spending time with my children. Playing with them, laughing, watching them have fun, learning together… These family moments are precious to me.

And it was during one of those very moments that the idea of **Kounouzi** was born.

### A game, bursts of laughter… and a small idea taking root.

One afternoon, during the holidays, we sat down as a family around a game.

The children were hooked straight away! They laughed, had fun, challenged each other… In short, we were having a great time.

But while we were playing, I couldn't stop thinking about something.

In that game, you had to buy shops, own businesses, answer questions…

And I said to myself:

*“But why couldn't they be maktabas, Umrah agencies or other places that mean more to us?”*

And the questions? Why couldn't they help children learn things that are truly beneficial for them?

After all, we are a Muslim family. Our religion is part of our daily life, our upbringing, our values.

**So why not find all of that in our moments of play too?**

Why not imagine a game where we have fun, laugh, challenge each other… while learning more about our religion and remembering Allah?

That's when it clicked.

### What if I created this game for my children?

As a web developer, I thought: *“After all, why not try to create our very own game for them?”*

At that point, I had absolutely no intention of selling it. I simply wanted to make my children happy and give them a game that reflects who we are.

So I got to work.

And one day, I showed them a very first version.

Well… let's be honest: it was far from perfect!

A robot voice, a very simple board, features still missing… It was a long way from the game you know today.

**But you know what? They loved it.**

And that, I really didn't expect.

They were completely into it! They wanted to answer the questions, take on the challenges, win… and above all, play again!

*“Dad, can we play another game?”*

Because of my work, I had to put the game aside for a while.

But they hadn't forgotten it.

Regularly, I would hear:

*“Dad, when are we playing Kounouzi again?”*

And believe me, when your children keep asking for a game you made yourself, it gives you quite a boost to finish it!

### Screens, yes… but not just any way.

Like many parents, we ask ourselves questions about how much time our children spend in front of screens.

And that is also one of the reasons Kounouzi meant so much to me.

I didn't want to create just another tablet game.

**I wanted that screen time to have meaning.**

That while playing, my children could learn their religion, discover new knowledge, develop their logic, think, do sums…

But above all, I wanted it to be a real moment of sharing.

Because with Kounouzi, we're not each in our own corner, eyes glued to a screen.

We play together.

We tease each other, we challenge each other, we laugh at the wrong answers and celebrate the right ones!

And sometimes, a simple question becomes the start of a lovely conversation.

A child doesn't understand an answer about our religion? We take the time to explain.

A maths question is giving them trouble? We work it out together.

A new idea comes up? We talk about it.

**In the end, the tablet is no longer what separates us. It becomes something that brings us together.**

And that's exactly what I was looking for.

### From our living room… to other families.

Seeing how much my children enjoyed Kounouzi, and above all the pleasure their mother and I took in playing with them, another idea began to grow.

*“What if other families could enjoy these same moments?”*

So we had friends and their children try the game.

And what a surprise!

The reactions were very similar.

The children had fun, asked for more games, wanted to play again and again.

And the parents were delighted to see their children enjoy a game so much that also gave them knowledge, especially about their religion.

That's when I realised that Kounouzi could go far beyond our little family circle.

This game I had imagined for my four children could perhaps bring joy to many other families, bi idhnillah.

### Behind the laughter, long hours of work.

Creating Kounouzi wasn't just about drawing a board and making up a few questions.

There were countless hours of development, research, checking and improvements.

One thing mattered to me in particular: **the seriousness of the educational content.**

For the religion questions, we wanted to rely on sources that can be identified and checked. It was essential to us that children don't just memorise an answer, but also understand what they are learning.

For maths, logic and the other subjects, we also relied on educational resources, including documents made by teachers.

The aim was simple: to offer a game that is fun, educational and carefully made.

And behind every improvement, there was always the same wish: to give children the game I would have loved to find for mine.

### Today, Kounouzi is for you too.

At first, Kounouzi was just a small idea born during the holidays, around a family game.

Today, it is a project we have chosen to share with you.

Because I am convinced that you can learn without being bored, pass things on without long speeches, and create wonderful memories around a simple game.

If Kounouzi helps children discover something new about their religion, helps parents share a lovely moment with them, or brings a family together around a game full of laughter and discovery, then all this work will have been worthwhile.

**Kounouzi was born for my children. Today, I hope it will find its place in your family too.**

May Allah put baraka in this project and make it a source of good for our children and yours.

**Abou Salmaan**

*Dad of four and creator of Kounouzi.*
